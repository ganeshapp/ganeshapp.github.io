A little detour

A fresh puzzle while you're here.

Generating your puzzle…

Tap a square, then a monster. Or drag a monster onto a square.

How to play

Colour the map with four colours, so that no two adjacent regions have the same colour. (Regions touching at only one corner do not count as adjacent.) There is a unique colouring consistent with the coloured regions you are already given.

Drag from a coloured region to a blank one to colour the latter the same colour as the former. Drag from outside the grid into a region to erase its colour. (You cannot change the colours of the regions you are given at the start of the game.)

Right-drag from a coloured region to a blank one to add dots marking the latter region as possibly the same colour as the former, or to remove those dots again.

How to play

Fill every empty square with a ghost, vampire or zombie. Match the monster totals above the grid. Each number around the edge tells you how many monsters you should see looking into that row or column, following any mirrors.

  • Ghosts are visible only in a mirror.
  • Vampires are visible only when viewed directly, without a mirror.
  • Zombies are always visible.

Tap a square, then a monster icon to fill it. You can also drag an icon onto a square. Use Erase to clear a square. Mirrors and monsters given at the start cannot be changed.

Turn on Pencil marks to mark possible monsters in an empty square. Tap the same monster again to remove its mark. Tap an edge number to mark it as done, or tap again to unmark it.
